What to fix, in order

Critical (2)

There is no tap-to-call phone number on the page. On a phone, that is the difference between a call and a back-button.

The fix: Add your phone number as a tap-to-call link near the top of the page.

The site never tells Google it is a local business (structured data). Competitors that do get preferred placement in the map results.

The fix: Add LocalBusiness structured data with your name, phone and address.

High (3)

Your page title is "Farha Roofing | Roofing Contractor | Commercial and Residential". It does not say what you do or where, which is exactly what Google shows searchers.

The fix: Rewrite the title to name your trade and city.

The server takes 2.8 seconds before the page even starts to appear. Visitors on phones give up in about 3.

The fix: Move to faster hosting or a modern site so the first response is quick.

Your name, address and phone are not written out as plain text, so AI tools and directories cannot verify who and where you are.

The fix: Write your business name, full address and phone as plain text in the footer.

Medium (6)

Most images have no text description, so Google image search cannot find your work.

The fix: Add a short alt description to each photo.

Your structured data is missing phone and address, so Google cannot verify your listing details.

The fix: Add telephone and address fields to your structured data.

The page loads 10 files before showing anything at all.

The fix: Reduce the number of stylesheets and scripts loaded before the page renders.

The page itself weighs 384 KB before a single image, heavy for mobile data.

The fix: Trim bloated page code so it loads fast on mobile data.

AI answers are built from question-and-answer content. Your site has none.

The fix: Add a short FAQ answering the questions customers actually ask.

Without structured business data, AI assistants have nothing reliable to quote about you.

The fix: Add LocalBusiness structured data (this also helps Google).

Low (3)

The fix: Give links meaningful wording, for example "See our roofing services".
