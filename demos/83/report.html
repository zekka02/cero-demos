What to fix, in order

Critical (2)

The site is not secure (no https). Browsers label it Not Secure and Google ranks it lower.

The fix: Install a free SSL certificate so the site loads over https.

The site never tells Google it is a local business (structured data). Competitors that do get preferred placement in the map results.

The fix: Add LocalBusiness structured data with your name, phone and address.

High (3)

The page has 3 main headlines (H1). Google wants exactly one.

The fix: Use one clear H1 headline naming your main service and area.

Your page title is "My WordPress |". It does not say what you do or where, which is exactly what Google shows searchers.

The fix: Rewrite the title to name your trade and city.

The site carries no structured data of any kind, the machine-readable label search engines rely on.

The fix: Add JSON-LD structured data describing your business.

Medium (7)

There is no page description for Google to show under your link, so Google invents one.

The fix: Add a one-sentence meta description of about 150 characters.

When your site is shared on WhatsApp or Facebook, no preview appears, just a bare link.

The fix: Add Open Graph title and description tags.

Most images have no text description, so Google image search cannot find your work.

The fix: Add a short alt description to each photo.

The page itself weighs 423 KB before a single image, heavy for mobile data.

The fix: Trim bloated page code so it loads fast on mobile data.

AI answers are built from question-and-answer content. Your site has none.

The fix: Add a short FAQ answering the questions customers actually ask.

Without structured business data, AI assistants have nothing reliable to quote about you.

The fix: Add LocalBusiness structured data (this also helps Google).

All photos use old heavy formats, slowing every page view.

The fix: Serve photos as WebP so they load faster.
